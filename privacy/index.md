# Privacy Policy — Backstage Release Plan

**Effective:** June 4, 2026

This is the privacy policy for Backstage Release Plan ("the app"), a mobile application that helps independent musicians plan music releases. The app is built and operated by Or Gilan ("we", "us").

## 1. The short version

The app works without an account. If you choose not to sign in, **no personal data ever leaves your device**. Your release plan, tasks, and any notes you write live in local storage on your phone.

You can optionally sign in with Apple or Google to back up and sync your data to our cloud. If you do, the data described below is stored on our servers.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not run third-party analytics or tracking inside the app.

## 2. What we collect, and only if you sign in

Signing in is optional and can be skipped. If you sign in:

- **Email address.** Provided by Apple or Google through their sign-in flows. Used solely to identify your account so we can sync your data across your devices.
- **Your release plan data.** This includes the title, type, and date of releases you create; the tasks the app generates or that you add; any text you type into tasks (notes, captions, contact handles); and the cover art images you choose for releases.
- **Crash and error reports.** If the app crashes or hits an error, we receive a technical report through Sentry that helps us fix the issue. These reports contain the error type, a stack trace, and your device model and operating system version. They do not contain your release content, contact details, or your email.

We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts list, location, audio recordings, microphone input, advertising identifier, or browsing behavior.

## 3. How your data is stored

- **On your device:** all app data is stored in a local database. This database stays on your phone unless you sign in for sync.
- **In the cloud (only if signed in):** synced data is stored in Supabase (a cloud database provider). Cover art images are stored in Supabase Storage. Both are protected by per-user row-level security so other users cannot access your data.
- **Crash reports:** sent to Sentry, retained according to Sentry's standard 90-day policy.

## 4. Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising. We do use the following services to operate the app:

- **Supabase** for database and storage (only if you sign in).
- **Apple and Google** as sign-in providers (only if you choose to sign in).
- **Sentry** for anonymous crash and error reporting.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms.

## 5. Your rights

- You can use the entire app without signing in.
- You can sign out at any time from Settings. Your local data stays on the device.
- You can delete a release inside the app, which also tombstones it in cloud sync so it is removed from our servers.
- You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing the address below.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will update the effective date and, if you are signed in, surface a notice inside the app on next launch.
